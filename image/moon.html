About the Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It is the brightest object in our night sky and plays an important role in Earth's tides. Scientists believe the Moon formed billions of years ago after a large object collided with the early Earth.

Interesting Facts About the Moon

  • Distance: The Moon is approximately 384,400 kilometers from Earth on average.
  • Diameter: The Moon is about 3,475 kilometers wide.
  • Orbit: It takes approximately 27.3 days to orbit Earth once relative to distant stars.
  • Phases: Its complete cycle of phases takes approximately 29.5 days.
  • Gravity: Gravity on the Moon is about one-sixth of Earth's gravity.
  • Surface: Its surface has many craters, mountains, and rocky plains.
  • Atmosphere: The Moon has an extremely thin exosphere rather than an atmosphere like Earth's.
